The first release of Duckwalk: Harold, a rubber debugging duck on your Flipper Zero, keeps your computer awake and works as its remote.

## Install

- **Flipper app:** download `duckwalk.fap` below and copy it to `apps/USB/` on the Flipper's SD card with qFlipper. Once Duckwalk is in the Flipper Apps Catalog, install it from the Flipper mobile app or Flipper Lab instead.
- **Desktop app (optional):** download the one for your computer and run it. Harold appears in the tray and lets go of the pointer while you use your own mouse or keyboard. It starts at login; its tray menu turns that off.
  - Windows: `duckwalk-desktop-windows-x64.exe` (most PCs) or `duckwalk-desktop-windows-arm64.exe` (Windows on ARM); it installs itself and joins the Start menu, so the download can go
  - macOS: `duckwalk-desktop-macos-arm64` (Apple silicon) or `duckwalk-desktop-macos-x64` (Intel); allow Input Monitoring when it asks
  - Linux: `duckwalk-desktop-linux-x64`; join the `input` group first ([how](https://github.com/arudaev/duckwalk/blob/main/desktop/app/README.md#linux-join-the-input-group))

Needs official Flipper firmware 1.5 or newer. Tested on Windows 11 over USB and Bluetooth; the macOS and Linux builds are untested, and reports are welcome.

## What's in it

- **Harold at his desk:** reads code, types, sips coffee, hunts bugs and quacks at them
- **Walk, Subtle and Wander** keep-awake modes
- **USB or Bluetooth**, with a connection check on the Down button
- **Settings:** how often, how far, work hours only, Stop after 1 to 8 hours
- **Remote:** media keys, slides and a D-pad mouse
- **Preview** of the next walk on the Up button

The [user guide](https://github.com/arudaev/duckwalk/blob/main/docs/guide.md) shows every screen.
